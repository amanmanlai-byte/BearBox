This is a geographic connection illustration, not traceroute or physical routing.
Locations come from online IP.SB responses; no offline MaxMind database is bundled.
Country counts use available public destination IPs, grouped by returned country code.
Missing coordinates do not erase known countries, but cannot provide precise map points.
Private/synthetic/missing destination IPs and failed queries remain explicitly unlocated.
IP geolocation is approximate; different destinations and IPv4/IPv6 may use different exits.
Day/night rendering uses device UTC time and approximate solar calculations:
https://gml.noaa.gov/grad/solcalc/calcdetails.html
It does not show real-time weather. Background is transparent.
